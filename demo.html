Two ways to sell

Close at the kitchen table, or from three counties away.

In the home

Present mode goes full screen on a tablet. Build the system in front of them, show the savings, and capture signatures before you leave.

  • Full-screen presentation, swipe or arrow keys
  • Works offline in basements and well houses
  • Signed agreement saved as a PDF on the spot

Remote

Send one link by text or email. They walk the same guided proposal on their phone, pick an option and sign. You get a signed link back.

  • “Let me think about it” gets the full walkthrough again
  • Their spouse can see the same proposal tonight
  • No app, no account, no login for the homeowner
